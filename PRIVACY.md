# Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 1, 2026

This is a personal, single-user tool used by its owner to edit videos. It connects to the owner's own Google Drive account to download source videos and upload finished edits.

## What the app accesses

The app accesses only the owner's own Google Drive files, and only to download videos to edit and upload the finished results.

## What the app stores

The app does not collect, sell, share, or transfer user data to any third party. Authorization tokens are stored only as private secrets in the owner's own development environment. Video files stay in the owner's Google Drive.

## Google API Services

Use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
